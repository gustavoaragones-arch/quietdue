What this estimate means

Early pregnancy dates are ranges, not exact points. Cycle length and ovulation timing vary, so estimates can shift—especially after the first ultrasound.

This tool gives a clear starting point, not a definitive date.

Early milestones (approximate)

  • Weeks 3–4: implantation window
  • Weeks 5–6: first heartbeat may be detectable
  • Weeks 7–9: first ultrasound often scheduled
How pregnancy dating works

Dating from the first day of your last period assumes ovulation about two weeks later; real ovulation moves with cycle length. Providers often refine dates after an ultrasound.

Frequently asked questions

Is QuietDue private?
Yes. Everything runs in your browser—no accounts, trackers, or cloud sync. Closing the page erases it.
Does this calculator save my data?
No. Nothing is saved or sent; your dates and results stay on your device only.
How accurate are early due dates?
Early dating is approximate: cycles and ovulation vary, and ultrasound can shift estimates. You get a range, not a single definitive date.
Can I use this without creating an account?
Yes. No sign-up is required.
Does QuietDue work offline?
Yes. After the first load, the calculator works without a network connection.
